Uma Oya soil and cover field data (Sri Lanka, October 2026): the data folder only

- 14 sites in and around the Uma Oya basin (Welimada, Ambewela and Perawela area, 1,000-1,900 m), 7 pairs: a farm or plantation next to a less-disturbed site.
- nir_scans.csv: soil pH, organic matter and carbon, ECEC, Ca, Mg, Fe, Al and moisture at 0, 10, 20 and 30 cm (55 scans).
  These are predictions from a handheld NIR scanner (AgroCares), NOT lab analyses. They were scanned field-moist.
- slake_peds.csv: aggregate stability, class 1-6, 18 peds per site.
- tape_points.csv / sites.csv: ground cover (plants, litter, rock, bare), canopy, slope and land use.
- Site positions are rounded to about 220 m because most sites are on private farms.
- README.md describes the full archive. The model, calibration and photo parts are not included here.

Unpublished. Please use it for your project but don't post the raw files publicly yet.
Cite as: Anderson, S. (2026) Uma Oya field cover, slake and NIR soil data, October 2026. University of Arizona.
