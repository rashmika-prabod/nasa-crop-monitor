Uma Oya data, October 2026
Sam Anderson, University of Arizona

soil_sites/   14 field sites, Oct 2026 (see READ_ME_FIRST.txt in there)
maps/         Uma Oya basin, GeoTIFF, WGS84, ~90 m pixels
tables/       basin-average rainfall by month and year (CHIRPS)
gee/          Earth Engine script for NASA data at a field

maps/
  elevation_m.tif               elevation (m), SRTM
  slope_deg.tif                 slope (degrees)
  landcover_worldcover.tif      ESA WorldCover 2021: 10 tree, 30 grass, 40 crop, 50 built, 60 bare, 80 water
  ndvi_median_2023_2026.tif     median Sentinel-2 NDVI 2023-2026
  ndvi_wobble_sd_2023_2026.tif  std dev of the same NDVI (planted/cleared fields swing more)
  cover_factor_C.tif            RUSLE C from NDVI, calibrated on the field sites
  erosion_t_ha_yr.tif           RUSLE soil loss (t/ha/yr), hillslopes only
  rain_erosivity_R.tif          RUSLE R (MJ mm/ha/h/yr)
  slope_length_LS.tif           RUSLE LS (capped at 100)
  soil_organic_carbon_g_kg.tif  OpenLandMap topsoil organic carbon (g/kg)
  act_here.tif                  1 = farm-like NDVI swing, 2 = same and well connected to streams
  basin / subbasins / streams   .geojson

Notes
- Soil values are from a handheld NIR scanner, not lab analyses. No N, P or K.
- Site positions are rounded to ~220 m (private farms).
- WorldCover misses a lot of the small vegetable plots.
- The erosion map is a model, so read it as more vs. less rather than exact numbers.
- The soil data isn't published yet, so please don't post the raw files publicly.
